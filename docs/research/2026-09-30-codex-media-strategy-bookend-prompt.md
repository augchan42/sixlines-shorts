## Part 2: the Goudy-on-ivory bookend

Every short today is green phosphor on black (the terminal look), and ends on a green end card: six
yang lines under SIX LINES, "REVEAL THE MOMENT", sixlines.day. The bookend (tried on 2 Kun, 33.1 s
total; frames in out/bookend/02-icon/frames/, t0.6 = open card, t32.0 = close card) wraps a finished
short:

- Open (0 to 1.2 s): ivory card (#FAF9F5, the website's colours), in Goudy Old Style: the label
  "2 · 坤 KŪN · THE RECEPTIVE" and the short's hook ("Tired of always having to lead?") large in the
  middle. At 1.2 s it flickers (two 3-frame flashes, 7 frames) into the green short, whose own hook
  is typing in at that moment. No sound on this flicker.
- Close: at the end the green end card flickers back to ivory, landing on the app icon's layout
  (the sixlines-ios app icon itself: SIX LINES in Goudy caps above six black yang bars), with
  "REVEAL THE MOMENT" and sixlines.day under it, held 1 s. Chopped TV static (a real recording,
  Pixabay) sounds only on the frames the screen changes. The short's music plays through.

The founder likes it ("Perfect. I love it when it's flickering into the app icon version.") and
the idea is that the brand is the ivory Goudy of the site and the app, and the terminal is the
look inside. The open question is the rollout: add it to the other week 1-2 shorts (Qian, Bo, Zhun,
Xu, Song) first, or render all 64.
