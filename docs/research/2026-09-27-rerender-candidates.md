## Why Wang Bi leaves out lines 1 and 6 (辯位)

From the text in chinese-classics-reference/zhouyi-zhushu/text/70-zhouyi-lüeli.md:

1. The Xiang never says a first or top line is in or out of its place (案象无初上得位失位之文).
2. The Xici, on places, speaks only of 3 with 5 and 2 with 4 (三五、二四同功異位), never of 1 or 6.
3. The texts contradict any fixed place for the top: the Wenyan says Qian's yang top line is
   "noble but without a place" (貴而无位), and the Xiang says Xu's yin top line is "not in its
   place" (雖不當位). If the top were a yang place, Qian's top would have one; if it were a yin
   place, Xu's top would have one. Both are said to have none.
4. So 1 and 6 are the start and the end of a matter (事之終始), not ranks. A place is a rank,
   "the ground where noble and humble are set out, the house that waits for ability to be used"
   (夫位者，列貴賤之地，待才用之宅也). Qian's line 1 is called "hidden" and its top "without a
   place": no one is hidden while holding office, or without a place while in it.
