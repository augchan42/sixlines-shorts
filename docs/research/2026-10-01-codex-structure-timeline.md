VERDICT: change.

Define the book and its patterns, hedge authorship, and correct what the dates refer to.

1. **Page 1**

   ```text
   q: HOW OLD IS THE I CHING
   a: A CHINESE BOOK ABOUT CHANGE.
      PARTS MAY BE 3,000 YEARS OLD.
   ```

   Reason: Introduces the book and makes its age approximate, without dating every layer alike. [Historical overview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780190072476_A42987815/preview-9780190072476_A42987815.pdf)

2. **Page 2**

   ```text
   q: LEGEND
   a: LEGEND CREDITS FU XI
      WITH EIGHT TRIGRAMS:
      THREE-LINE PATTERNS.
   ```

   Reason: Explains “trigram” and keeps the attribution explicitly legendary.

3. **Page 3**

   ```text
   q: 11TH CENTURY BC
   a: TRADITION CREDITS KING WEN
      WITH A TEXT FOR EACH OF THE
      64 SIX-LINE PATTERNS.
   ```

   Reason: Uses King Wen’s traditional period and distinguishes the texts attributed to him from the whole book. [Historical overview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780190072476_A42987815/preview-9780190072476_A42987815.pdf)

4. **Page 4**

   ```text
   q: 11TH CENTURY AD
   a: 64 PATTERNS, SQUARE AND CIRCLE.
      ATTRIBUTED TO SHAO YONG.
   ```

   Reason: Hedges authorship because the surviving printed diagrams appear later. [Repo research](docs/research/2026-10-01-codex-guide-leibniz.md)

5. **Page 5**

   ```text
   q: 1701
   a: BOUVET SENDS THE DIAGRAM
      TO LEIBNIZ, A MATHEMATICIAN.
   ```

   Reason: Connects the diagram to the preceding page and identifies Leibniz; 1701 dates the sending. [Bouvet’s letter](https://archive.swarthmore.edu/leibniz-bouvet/letters/letter-i-4-november-1701-bouvet-to-leibniz/)

6. **Page 6**

   ```text
   q: 1950
   a: BAYNES'S ENGLISH VERSION
      OF WILHELM'S TRANSLATION
      APPEARS WITH JUNG'S FOREWORD.
   ```

   Reason: Dates publication, credits Baynes, and avoids saying Jung wrote the foreword in 1950. [Edition history](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780190072476_A42987815/preview-9780190072476_A42987815.pdf)

7. **Page 7**

   ```text
   q: 1962 // PUBLISHED
   a: THE MAN IN THE HIGH CASTLE.
      PHILIP K. DICK USED THE I CHING
      TO HELP PLOT THE NOVEL.
   ```

   Reason: Separates publication from writing and describes the book’s role without implying it supplied the entire plot. [Dick’s account](https://philipdick.com/literary-criticism/interviews/hour-25-a-talk-with-philip-k-dick/)

8. **Page 8**

   ```text
   q: TODAY
   a: SIX LINES READS THE BOOK
      TO THINK THROUGH A CHOICE.
   ```

   Reason: Brings the timeline to the present and states the app’s use without promising an outcome.
