VERDICT: change.

The voices and jaw movement make the speakers clear. The joke works: Urizen boasts about continuing; the narrator exposes his inability to stop.

1. **Urizen:** I am Urizen. I measure everything.
2. **Urizen:** Six solid lines. Heaven never rests. Neither do I.
3. **Urizen:** The dragon rose. The dragon flew. And I keep climbing.
4. **Guide:** The I Ching. Qian, top line: the dragon that went too high. There is regret.
5. **Guide:** An old commentary explains: it knows how to advance, not how to withdraw.
6. **Guide:** William Blake’s Urizen makes rules for everything.
7. **Urizen:** Withdraw?

**65 words:** approximately 36–38 seconds at 130–140 words per minute, including the specified gaps and closing card.

Reasons for changes:

- **Beat 2:** “Solid” describes the visible lines; “Neither do I” makes his refusal to rest the boast.
- **Beat 4:** Names the I Ching for newcomers and connects “top line” to the graphic; “There is regret” sounds more natural than “It has regret.”
- **Beat 6:** “Warning” is a defensible interpretation, but overstates a single intention for a complex figure; rule-making fits Urizen’s limiting reason and law. [Blake Archive](https://blog.blakearchive.org/2018/01/31/urizen-bound-and-unbound/)

“The dragon that went too high” fairly renders 亢龍 in this reading: excessive height and overextension, rather than merely flying high. “An old commentary explains” accurately introduces the Wenyan, one of the Ten Wings. [Qian text and commentary](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/周易正義/01乾), [Wenyan passage](https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=gb&node=278964&show=parallel)

“What is full cannot last” fairly conveys 盈不可久也 in context, but keep the Wenyan here: it describes the failure of judgment and supplies the word for the ending. Adding both would crowd the short. [Small Image](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/周易正義/01乾)

Keep **“Withdraw?”** Deliver it as baffled disbelief, with the sun extinguishing immediately. Let it be the last spoken word. On screen, use **“KNOWS HOW TO ADVANCE, NOT HOW TO WITHDRAW.”**
