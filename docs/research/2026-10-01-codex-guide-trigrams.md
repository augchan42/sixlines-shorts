The chicken ending works. Keep the delivery even. The draft needs a clearer introduction, an identified commentary, and a reason to read the completion example.

1. **“The I-Ching. A book of situations. Each six-line figure has two three-line halves, called trigrams.”**  
   Picture: THE I-CHING types onto the device. One six-line figure separates into two halves; label each TRIGRAM.  
   Reason: Tells a newcomer what the book is before explaining its structure.

2. **“There are eight: every combination of solid and broken lines.”**  
   Picture: Show a solid line and a broken line, labelled in English. Eight trigrams form a stationary grid; type 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.  
   Reason: Names the two line types instead of leaving the viewer to infer them.

3. **“One commentary lists their qualities: heaven, strong. Earth, yielding. Thunder, moving. Wind, entering.”**  
   Picture: Type 說卦 Discussion of the Trigrams. Show four trigram cards: 乾 HEAVEN / STRONG; 坤 EARTH / YIELDING; 震 THUNDER / MOVING; 巽 WIND / ENTERING.  
   Reason: Introduces the source; all four pairings are supported by the [text](</Users/auchan/projects/chinese-classics-reference/zhouyi-kouyi/text/13-shuogua.md:125>).

4. **“Water, danger. Fire, clinging. Mountain, stopping. Lake, joy.”**  
   Picture: Replace the four cards with 坎 WATER / DANGER; 離 FIRE / CLINGING; 艮 MOUNTAIN / STOPPING; 兌 LAKE / JOY.  
   Reason: “Danger” conveys the peril in 坎陷; “sinking” sounds like a physical action and obscures the quality.

5. **“It also lists animals: horse, ox, dragon, chicken, pig, pheasant, dog, sheep.”**  
   Picture: Return to the eight-trigram grid. Add simple animal outlines beside their corresponding trigrams as they are named; distinguish the pheasant with a long tail. Give the chicken equal size and treatment.  
   Reason: Keeps the accurate list and lets its oddness carry the humour; making the chicken smaller and last announces the joke.

6. **“Fire below water: After Completion. The task: guard against trouble.”**  
   Picture: Stack the fire trigram below water. Type 63 · 既濟 After Completion, then GUARD AGAINST TROUBLE.  
   Reason: The arrangement is correct; the added instruction conveys the [Great Image’s warning](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/周易/既濟), rather than leaving “already across” sounding like a favourable result.

7. **“Recommended for: reading figures in halves. Not recommended for: arguing with the chicken.”**  
   Picture: Type both summary lines beside a tick and cross. Hold the same chicken outline beside the second line, motionless. Then the existing sixlines.day close.  
   Reason: Avoids the unexplained word “hexagram”; the chicken callback lands without animation telling viewers to laugh.
