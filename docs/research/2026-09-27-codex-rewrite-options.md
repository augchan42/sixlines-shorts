## Five difficult rewrites

These are English applications of the supplied texts, not quotations or proposed literal translations.

### 1 — The Creative

The main problem is the existing “No conditions. Just mean it.” It removes the attention to timing found throughout the supplied lines. “Sign” in everyday speech does not necessarily mean an omen; that word alone is not a reason to reject a draft.

| Part | Draft |
|---|---|
| Hook | Waiting to feel ready? |
| Meaning 1 | The Creative: / strength takes work. |
| Meaning 2 | Keep practising. / Stay alert. |
| Question | What can you / work on today? |
| Lesson | **Build your strength. / Choose when to act.** |

Finding, line 3:

> LINE 3: ACTIVE ALL DAY.  
> STILL ALERT AT NIGHT.

Basis: the Great Image's 自強不息 and line 3's 終日乾乾，夕惕若厲; the supplied Wang Bi note adds 因時而惕，不失其幾. The point is sustained effort with attention to timing. Avoid turning it into a demand to work without rest.

### 2 — The Receptive

Keep the existing hook, meaning cards and question. Replace only the lesson:

> **Help see it through.**  
> **You needn't lead it.**

Finding, line 3:

> LINE 3: SERVES THE WORK.  
> FOLLOWS IT THROUGH TO THE END.

Basis: 无成有終 and Wang Bi's 不為事主，順命而終. This makes supporting an active responsibility. “Keep your shine in” is less clear and can suggest hiding one's ability.

### 3 — Difficulty at the Beginning

The current lesson is already useful. A modest revision is enough:

> **Keep your aim.**  
> **Get help before moving.**

Finding, line 1:

> AT THE BASE: IT PAUSES.  
> ITS AIM REMAINS STEADY.

Basis: 磐桓，利居貞，利建侯 and 雖磐桓，志行正也. “Steady” is an accessible application of the passage; 正 also concerns the correctness of the aim. The lesson connects the pause to maintaining direction and gathering support. Keep the hook and question. Replace **Boring beats / bold here** with **Get support / before you move** if adopting this emphasis.

### 5 — Waiting

Keep the setup and rain/rest animation. Replace the lesson:

> **Rest while you wait.**  
> **Keep your strength.**

Basis: the Great Image's 飲食宴樂 and the Tuan's danger ahead and strength that does not fall into it, 險在前也，剛健而不陷. “Keep your strength” is the practical application. The character can still stand in the rain and lie down after it. No promise that waiting will produce the desired result; no instruction that the viewer must stop feeling worried.

### 48 — The Well — approved

The user’s reading: a shared resource that requires maintenance and outlasts the people around it. The user approved the following direction and wording. This replaces the two earlier options.

| Part | Approved wording |
|---|---|
| Hook | Who maintains what everyone uses? |
| Meaning | People come and go. / The well remains. |
| Question | What do you help / keep in working order? |
| Lesson | **Care for what / others depend on.** |

The meaning is recorded as the single two-line statement approved in conversation. Its allocation to the template’s two meaning cards remains to be worked out.

The text connects continuity, maintenance and shared use:

- **Continuity:** 改邑不改井 — the settlement changes; the well remains. “Outlasts the people around it” is the modern application.
- **Maintenance:** line 1 describes a muddy, neglected well; line 2 a leaking vessel; line 3 a cleaned well; line 4 repairs its lining. Its Small Image says 脩井也, repairing the well.
- **Shared use:** line 6 says 勿幕, do not cover it. Wang Bi explains 不擅其有，不私其利: do not keep possession or benefit private.

Keep the existing well-frame and water animation. The text explains the care needed to keep that resource useful to others. No production copy or render has been changed for this approval.

## Other edits worth keeping small

- 7's **Lead upright, so the ranks can be** is unfinished English. Try **Set the example / you ask them to follow.**
- 16's **Move with them, not at them** is difficult to interpret. Try **Give the group / a shared purpose.** Treat it as an application rather than a translation.
- 47's **Words fail here. Let actions speak** retains an absolute first sentence. Try **When words fail, / show your resolve.**
- 58's **Joy is meant to be shared** is understandable but unnecessary if the lesson already says **Laugh and learn / with friends.** Keep that concrete social action central.
- Do not replace every definite sentence with “may.” Remove unjustified guarantees while keeping the advice direct.
