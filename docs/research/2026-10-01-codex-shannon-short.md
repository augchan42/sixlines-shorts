The arithmetic is right, with conditions:

- **Coins:** three independent fair coins give line values 6/7/8/9 with odds 1/8, 3/8, 3/8, 1/8. Yin and yang each total 1/2. [Probability derivation](https://www.thekimerers.net/brian/iching/stalk.html).
- **Yarrow:** the standard model gives 1/16, 5/16, 7/16, 3/16, so yin and yang each total 8/16. It assumes equally likely remainders when counting by fours; physical splitting does not guarantee that distribution. [Model and assumptions](https://experiments.theoriginaliching.com/en/experimentos/ritual-milenrama).
- **Six bits:** always enough to encode the broken/solid pattern. Six bits of Shannon entropy requires all 64 patterns to be equally likely. It excludes moving-line status and says nothing about the reading’s usefulness. [Shannon’s 1948 paper](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/shannon/entropy/entropy.pdf).
