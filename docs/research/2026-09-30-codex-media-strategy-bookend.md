   Correct these specific claims:

   - **“A few Shorts a month is enough.”** Enough for what? Buffer’s frequency guide says **one to three Shorts a week**.    - **TikTok’s ordered list of ranking factors.** TikTok confirms that interactions, including watching and skipping, matter heavily. [YouTube metric definitions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9313698?hl=en-GB)
