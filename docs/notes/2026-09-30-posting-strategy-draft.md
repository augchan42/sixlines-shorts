## Draft plan

**Week 0: warm-up.** grokbot uses the three accounts by hand for a week (engagement-style.md). No
posts. Meanwhile: video hosting for Buffer, YouTube titles, and the board review of 27-64.

**Weeks 1 onward: three a week on each of Instagram, TikTok, YouTube.** The same video on all three
the same week, in each channel's own slots.

- Two of the three: the 64 shorts in King Wen order, 1 to 64. 32 weeks.
- The third: a feature, rotating: a character lesson, a special, a structure short, a lesson cut.
  The features run out of made material around week 20 unless we keep making them.

**Pinned on each profile:** the readout key or the Wang Bi teaser, so a new visitor learns how to
read the shorts.

**X and Threads (Typefully):** the same shorts, plus the long lessons in full as threads.

**Seasonal:** winter solstice 2026-12-21 (24 復 Return, the light coming back, a character lesson
exists); Lunar New Year 2027-02-06.

## Gaps

1. Video hosting: Buffer takes a public URL, not an upload.
2. YouTube needs a title per short; TikTok takes one too. We have Instagram and LinkedIn captions only.
3. Long lessons: the Wang Bi lesson (228 s) is over YouTube Shorts' 3 minutes.
4. A profile intro: nothing says in 20 s what Six Lines is, for the pinned slot.
5. Covers: each platform shows a cover frame in the grid; we have not chosen them.
6. The AI label: Buffer has an isAiGenerated flag for all three platforms.
