## Points to review

- **Shortened names:** some long names are shortened on screen: Decay (18), Great Excess (28), Dark times (36), Completion (63), Gathering (45). Hexagram 62 uses the text's own "Small things: yes. Big things: no." instead of its name.
- **Harsh judgments:** 12, 23 and 54 have the harshest judgments in the book. The copy is practical, not grim.
- **Hexagram 44:** the source is about a "strong maiden" who shouldn't be married. The copy leaves the gender out and keeps the warning.

## Beats and transitions by music class

The timeline stays the same: every section stays on the same beat, counted from the drop. Only the cuts and camera change with the energy class of the upper trigram's track (classes from `docs/research/2026-09-24-transitions.md`).

| Class | Tracks (shorts) | Before the drop | On the drop | Screens | End card |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| calm | kun, gen (16) | Slow dissolves of 1–1.5 beats, no whips | Zoom only, no shake | Cross-fade | join |
| steady | qian, li (16) | One cut per bar, light punch | Zoom + small shake | Single-line wipe (new Blender clip with alpha) | flip |
| building | zhen, kan, xun (24) | Dissolves from the hook to the meaning, then whips from the question on | Glitch + flash + full shake (today's look) | Whips | snap |
| driving | dui (8) | Whips throughout | Glitch + full shake | Glitch cuts | snap |

- **New work:** the single-line wipe is the only new piece. Everything else is already built. If the wipe doesn't look good, steady uses a slide instead.
- **Re-renders:** the 8 finished shorts get re-rendered under their class so the series stays consistent.
- **Soundtrack variety:** zhen, xun and dui each have a second usable drop (`music/drops.json`). If the same 20 s of music gets repetitive, half of each of those tracks' shorts could use the other drop. Not planned unless it's heard as a problem.
