You are rating the on-screen copy of 56 short vertical videos (Instagram Reels, YouTube
Shorts), one per I-Ching hexagram, made to promote an I-Ching app. You know nothing else
about the project.

The brief you are given lays out each short as a viewer meets it: every piece of text at
the second it appears, for as long as it stays, with its real line breaks, then the post
caption under the video. Read each short in that order, at that pace, as someone scrolling
who has never heard of the hexagram. Text you can't take in within the time it is on
screen is lost.

Score each short 1 to 10 overall, judging:
- Hook: would it stop you? Is it about a real situation a viewer has?
- Meaning: the two lines after the hexagram. Clear, specific, true to the hexagram's name,
  readable in the time given.
- Question: does it make you think of your own life, or want to comment?
- Caption: plain, 1 to 2 sentences, adds something the video doesn't say.
- The whole: do hook, meaning and question make one idea, in the order they arrive?
The app screens, end card and credit are the same in every short; don't score them.

Use the whole scale. Don't give everything 7. Be honest.

Write a JSON list to the output path you are given, in the brief's order, each item:
{"number": 3, "score": 7, "best": "the strongest part, a few words", "weakest": "the weakest part and why, one sentence"}
Write nothing else to disk.
