# Early character forms for the 64 hexagram names

Research for a new kind of lesson: the hexagram's character is drawn stroke by stroke in 3D, then one plain sentence appears. Each entry in `research.json` gives the oldest attested form, the scholarly reading with a confidence level, our own reading of what the shape can show, a drawing idea, a sentence and a fit score. For two-character names, `picture_char` is the character that carries the picture.

## Summary

- Fit: 5 at 5, 16 at 4, 27 at 3, 14 at 2, 2 at 1. 21 of 64 are at 4 or 5.
- Confidence: 23 established, 25 disputed, 16 folk.
- "folk" is used two ways: the popular picture story is not supported (遯 as a fleeing piglet, 觀 as a staring stork, 家 as a pig under a roof is now marked disputed), or the character is a sound part plus a category part (言, 手, 女, 水, 艸, 辵...) and the category part does not show the hexagram's point. For those, the drawing rests on our reading, not on the scholarship.
- The strongest pictures are ones where the shape and the lesson are the same thing: 屯 a sprout pushing through the ground, 蠱 worms breeding in a closed bowl, 恆 the moon between two lines, 益 water running over a bowl, 困 a tree inside walls.
- Weakest (fit 1-2): mostly phonetic compounds, such as 損 and 姤, where only our own reading gives a picture.
- `commons_svg` points to Wikimedia Commons files of the form `File:<char>-oracle.svg` (or bronze, bigseal, seal), checked with the Commons API. 8 have none: 坤, 訟, 剝, 遯, 睽, 損, 姤, 渙. A few link to a component instead: 震 uses 辰, 大過 and 大壯 use 大.
- Every `our_reading` starts with "Ours:" and is our own idea, not scholarship.
